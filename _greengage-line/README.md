# Greengage Line marketing pipeline

Source for the turning-day cards and signage. Moved here from
`Design iCloud/green days/marketing/` during the September 2026 consolidation.

- `turning-day-card-TEMPLATE.html` plus the numbered card files, driven by
  `turning-days.json`
- `plates-norm-15.py` normalizes the plate images
- `greengage-line-signage.html` and `greengage-line-second-opinion.html`

The PNGs this system produces are output, not source. They live in
`~/w/assets/green-days/`, along with the processed produce illustrations
(`produce/`), the raw Midjourney generations (`produce_raw/`), the delivered
Instagram images (`ig/`), the signage package zip, and the turning-day 14
critique package.
