Privacy

Last updated 28 September 2026

The short version. Green Days has no accounts, no cookies and no advertising. It doesn't know who you are and has no way to find out. What you choose in the app (your market, how you eat, your basket) stays on your device, except when you ask for a recipe. The analytics we keep count what happens in the app, not who did it.

Who runs this

Green Days is made by Ryan Nance in Lisbon, Portugal, who is responsible for how your information is handled. Questions or requests: hello@greendays.day.

What stays on your device

The app saves a few things in your browser's local storage so it remembers you between visits: your market, how you eat and anything you avoid, your basket, recent recipes, a local grower if you picked one, whether you've finished the welcome screens, the date of your last visit, and the produce you asked to be reminded about. None of it is sent to us as a record of you. You can erase it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

When you ask for a recipe

To write a recipe, the app sends our server your basket, your market, the date, how you eat, anything you avoid, and up to three things you've told it are already in your kitchen. Our server passes that to Anthropic, whose Claude model writes the recipe. The request carries no name, account or identifier. Identical requests on the same day may be answered from a short-lived cache so the same recipe isn't written twice.

What we count

The app reports simple events to our server: that it opened, which screen was shown, that a recipe was made, which produce page was viewed, that a button was tapped. Each event carries the market you're using and a random number that lives only as long as the page is open, so events from one visit can be grouped together. It is never stored on your device and it changes every time you reload. From your connection, our host adds the country you're visiting from. The first event of a visit also says whether this device has used Green Days before and how many days ago. We never record what you type into search, and our analytics don't record IP addresses.

These counts are kept in Cloudflare's Analytics Engine for no more than three months and are only ever read in aggregate.

Reminders

"Remind me when it's back" gives you a calendar file for the month a season opens. It's made when you tap and nothing about it is stored. Once it's in your calendar, your calendar does the reminding.

Services that see your connection

  • Cloudflare hosts the site. Like any host it sees your IP address to deliver pages, briefly rate-limits recipe requests by IP, keeps short operational logs of requests, and measures page load speed with its cookieless Web Analytics. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts serves the app's typefaces, so your browser requests them from Google, which sees your IP address when it does. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Anthropic writes recipes from the details listed above.
  • Delivery partners. In some markets the app links to a produce delivery service through an affiliate network. If you follow that link, the partner's and the network's own policies apply from then on.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask what we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. Because Green Days keeps no record tied to a person, there is usually nothing to find, and what the app remembers lives on your device where you can clear it yourself. If you think something is wrong, write to the address above, or complain to the Portuguese data protection authority, the CNPD.

Changes

If what Green Days collects changes, this page changes first, and the date at the top moves with it.
