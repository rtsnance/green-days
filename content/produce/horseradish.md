*Rábano-picante* in Portugal, *raifort* in France from Old French *raiz fort*, strong root, and *peberrod* in Denmark, *pepparrot* in Sweden — pepper-root. German *Meerrettich* is not sea-radish; *Meer* there carries its older sense of foreign. Most wasabi served outside Japan is this root, dyed green and cut with mustard, and the two are cousins in the cabbage family. A whole root smells of nothing. Grating breaks the cells and starts the reaction that makes the heat, and vinegar stops it — add it at once for mild, wait about three minutes for fierce. Simplest thing to do with it: grate it raw over hot beef.
