*Nabo* in Portugal and Spain, *rapa* in Italy — and in Portugal the root is frequently not the point at all. The young leaves are sold on their own as *nabiças* and the flowering tops as *grelos*, both of which turn up in soup and alongside pork far more often than the white root does. Buy small either way: much bigger than a fist and it goes woody and harshly peppery. Simplest thing to do with it: quartered and roasted hard until the cut edges catch, which trades the pepper for sweetness.
